Pearson Correlation

The correlation is the covariance with the units divided out: rescale by the standard deviation of x and the standard deviation of y. Equivalently, it is the covariance of the two z-scored variables. In expanded form, the hidden running index i still pairs each x sub i with its y sub i, across all n cases.

Read aloud: r sub x y equals s sub x y over s sub x times s sub y.
