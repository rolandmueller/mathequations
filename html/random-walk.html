A random walk adds up random steps. The position after n steps is the sum of the individual steps, each +1 or −1 with equal chance, like a running tally of coin flips. On average the walker goes nowhere, but the typical distance from the start grows like the square root of the number of steps: after 100 steps, about 10 away; after 10000, about 100.

Read aloud: S n equals the sum from i equals one to n of X i. The standard deviation of S n equals the square root of n.
