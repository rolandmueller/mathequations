Residual Sum of Squares

The residual sum of squares is the loss function of linear regression. For every observation, take the actual response, subtract the line's prediction (the intercept plus the slope times the predictor), square the gap, and add up. Least squares picks the line that makes this as small as possible.

Read aloud: R S S equals the sum from i equals one to n of y sub i minus beta zero hat minus beta one hat times x sub i, squared.
