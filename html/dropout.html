Dropout

Dropout fights overfitting in neural networks by randomly switching off neurons during training. Each activation is multiplied by a random mask that is 0 with probability p and 1 otherwise, drawn fresh for every training step. Survivors are scaled up so the expected output stays the same. No neuron can rely on any particular other neuron being present.

Read aloud: a tilde j equals m j over one minus p times a j, where m j is Bernoulli with success probability one minus p.
