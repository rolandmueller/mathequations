Minimizing the residual sum of squares has a closed-form answer. The slope is how x and y co-vary around their means x bar and y bar, divided by how much x varies on its own, both summed over all observations. The intercept then makes the line pass through the point of means.

Read aloud: beta one hat equals the sum of x sub i minus x bar times y sub i minus y bar, over the sum of x sub i minus x bar squared. Beta zero hat equals y bar minus beta one hat times x bar.
