Mean Squared Error

The mean squared error is the average over all observations of the squared gap between the true response and the model's prediction. Computed on the data the model was trained on, it is the training error; computed on held-out data, it is the test error, which is what actually matters.

Read aloud: M S E equals one over n times the sum from i equals one to n of y sub i minus f hat of x sub i, squared.

Definitions

mse

Mean squared error MSE\mathrm{MSE}MSE. Pronounced: "M S E". Training MSE always falls as a model gets more flexible; test MSE first falls, then rises again: the U-shape of overfitting. The model with the lowest training error is rarely the one with the lowest test error. Its square root, RMSE, is in the unit of yyy.
