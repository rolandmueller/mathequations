Local explanation methods break one prediction into pieces. Start from a baseline, the average prediction, then add up one contribution per feature, positive or negative. The pieces add up exactly to the prediction, so you can say "€40k above average: +€55k for location, −€15k for age".

Read aloud: f hat of x equals phi zero plus the sum from j equals one to p of phi j.
