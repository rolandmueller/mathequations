Permutation Test of Correlation

Is an observed correlation more than chance? If x and y were unrelated, any pairing of the y values with the x values would be equally plausible. So shuffle y, compute the correlation of the shuffled pairs, and count how often it reaches the observed correlation. That share, over B shuffles and divided by B, is the p-value.

Read aloud: p hat equals one over B times the sum from b equals one to B of the indicator that r of x and y permuted by pi b is at least r of x and y.
