An A/B test compares the conversion rate of version A with the conversion rate of version B. The z-score divides their difference by its standard error, built from the pooled variance of a single yes/no outcome and the sizes of group A and group B.

Read aloud: z equals p hat A minus p hat B, over the square root of p hat times one minus p hat times one over n A plus one over n B.
