p-Value

The p-value is the probability of getting a test statistic at least as extreme as the one actually observed, assuming the null hypothesis is true. A small p-value means the data would be surprising in a world where nothing is going on.

Read aloud: p equals the probability that the absolute value of T is at least the absolute value of t observed, given H zero.
