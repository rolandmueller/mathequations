With more than two classes, each class gets its own linear score. The softmax exponentiates each score to make it positive and divides by the sum over all classes of the exponentiated scores, so the class probabilities are positive and add up to 1.

Read aloud: the probability that Y equals k given x equals e to the z k, over the sum from l equals one to K of e to the z l.
