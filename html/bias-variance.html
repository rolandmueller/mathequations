The expected test error at a new point splits into three parts. Variance: how much the fitted model would change if trained on a different sample. Squared bias: how far the average fitted model is from the truth. Irreducible noise: randomness no model can predict. Flexible models have low bias but high variance; rigid models the reverse. The best model balances the two.

Read aloud: the expected value of y zero minus f hat of x zero, squared, equals the variance of f hat of x zero plus the squared bias of f hat of x zero plus the variance of epsilon.
