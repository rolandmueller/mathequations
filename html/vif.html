Variance Inflation Factor

Collinearity: when predictors are strongly related to each other, the model cannot tell their effects apart. The variance inflation factor measures how much this inflates the variance of a coefficient. It comes from the R squared of predicting X j from all the other predictors: if the others already explain X j well, there is little independent information left to estimate its coefficient.

Read aloud: the V I F of beta j hat equals one over one minus R squared of X j given all other X.
