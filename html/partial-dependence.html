A partial dependence plot shows how a model's prediction changes with one feature, on average. Fix the feature of interest at a value, keep each observation's other features as they are, ask the model for a prediction, and average over all observations. Repeating this across a grid of values traces the partial dependence curve.

Read aloud: f hat S of x S equals one over n times the sum from i equals one to n of f hat of x S and x C i.
