Sample Covariance

Covariance asks whether two variables move together. For each case, multiply how far x is from its mean by how far y is from its mean. If both are above or both are below their means, the product is positive. Add these products up and average with n − 1.

Read aloud: s sub x y equals one over n minus one times the sum from i equals one to n of x sub i minus x bar, times y sub i minus y bar.

Definitions

cov

Sample covariance sxys_{xy}sxy​. Pronounced: "s sub x y", or "the covariance of x and y". Also written cov(x,y)\mathrm{cov}(x, y)cov(x,y), read "cov of x and y". The subscript xyxyxy names the two variables involved, not an index. The covariance is positive when xxx and yyy tend to deviate in the same direction, negative when they deviate in opposite directions, and near zero when there is no linear association. Its unit is the product of the two units, so its magnitude is hard to interpret. That is the motivation for the correlation coefficient. Note that sxx=sx2s_{xx} = s_x^2sxx​=sx2​: variance is the covariance of a variable with itself.
