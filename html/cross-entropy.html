Cross-Entropy Loss

Cross-entropy is the standard loss for classification. For each observation, only the true class counts, and we take the log of the probability the model gave it. Confident correct predictions cost almost nothing; confident wrong ones cost a lot. The loss is the negative of these logs, averaged over observations and summed over classes.

Read aloud: L equals minus one over n times the sum over i and the sum over k of y i k times the log of p hat i k.
