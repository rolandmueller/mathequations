Sample Variance

The variance measures spread. Take each observation, see how far it lies from the mean, square that distance, add them all up, and average over n − 1 rather than n.

Read aloud: s squared equals one over n minus one times the sum from i equals one to n of x sub i minus x bar, squared.

Definitions

variance

Sample variance s2s^2s2. Pronounced: "s squared". The average squared distance from the mean. Its unit is the square of the data's unit (e.g. cm² for heights in cm), which is why the standard deviation is usually reported instead. The population variance is written σ2\sigma^2σ2 ("sigma squared", lowercase sigma).

bessel

Bessel's correction 1n−1\frac{1}{n-1}n−11​. Pronounced: "one over n minus one". nnn is the sample size. The deviations are measured from xˉ\bar{x}xˉ, which was fitted to the same data and therefore sits closer to them than the true mean μ\muμ ("mu") does. Dividing by n−1n-1n−1 makes s2s^2s2 an unbiased estimator of σ2\sigma^2σ2. The quantity n−1n-1n−1 is the number of degrees of freedom left after estimating xˉ\bar{x}xˉ. Dividing by nnn instead gives the maximum-likelihood estimator under normality. It is biased, but has a lower mean squared error.

sum

Summation ∑i=1n\sum_{i=1}^{n}∑i=1n​. Pronounced: "the sum from i equals one to n". The running index iii steps through 1,2,…,n1, 2, \dots, n1,2,…,n, so each observation contributes one squared deviation. nnn is the sample size.

value

A single observation xix_ixi​. Pronounced: "x sub i". The iii-th data point. As iii runs from 111 to nnn, this visits every observation once.

mean

Sample mean xˉ\bar{x}xˉ. Pronounced: "x bar". The reference point from which deviations are measured. It has no subscript because it is the same for every iii. Without squaring, ∑(xi−xˉ)=0\sum (x_i - \bar{x}) = 0∑(xi​−xˉ)=0 always.

square

Squaring, the exponent 222. Pronounced: "squared". Makes every deviation positive so they cannot cancel. It also weights large deviations disproportionately, which is why variance is outlier-sensitive.
