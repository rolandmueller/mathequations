Logistic regression is a linear model, but for the log of the odds, not for the probability itself. The log-odds equal an intercept plus a coefficient times the predictor. So each coefficient says how the log-odds change, which is why interpreting them takes practice.

Read aloud: the log of p of X over one minus p of X equals beta zero plus beta one times X.
