Cost-Complexity Pruning

A fully grown tree overfits. Cost-complexity pruning searches the subtrees of the big tree for the best trade-off between fitting the training data, the RSS summed over all leaves, and tree size, the number of leaves, with alpha as the price per leaf. Small alpha keeps the big tree; large alpha cuts it down to a stump.

Read aloud: minimize over subtrees T of T zero the sum over leaves m and observations in R m of y i minus y hat R m, squared, plus alpha times the number of leaves of T.
