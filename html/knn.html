To classify a new point, KNN looks at its K closest training points and lets them vote. The estimated probability of class j is the fraction of those neighbors whose label equals j. The prediction is the class with the most votes.

Read aloud: the probability that Y equals j given X equals x zero equals one over K times the sum over i in N zero of the indicator that y sub i equals j.
