The bell curve. Its density is highest at the mean and falls off symmetrically as the value moves away, measured in units of the standard deviation. The exponential of minus half the squared z-score gives the bell shape; the normalizing constant makes the total area equal 1.

Read aloud: f of x equals one over sigma root two pi times e to the minus one half of x minus mu over sigma, squared.
