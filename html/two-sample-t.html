Two-Sample t-Test (Welch)

The t-statistic compares the mean of group 1 with the mean of group 2. The difference is divided by its standard error, which combines each group's variance over its size with the other group's variance over its size. Noisy or small groups make the same difference less convincing.

Read aloud: t equals x bar one minus x bar two, over the square root of s one squared over n one plus s two squared over n two.
