Residual Standard Error

The residual standard error is the typical size of a prediction miss, in the units of the response. Take the residual sum of squares, average it over the degrees of freedom left after fitting, and take the square root.

Read aloud: R S E equals the square root of one over n minus p minus one times R S S.

Definitions

rse

Residual standard error RSE\mathrm{RSE}RSE. Pronounced: "R S E". An estimate of σ\sigmaσ, the standard deviation of the error term ε\varepsilonε. It measures lack of fit in absolute terms, while R2R^2R2 measures it relative to the total variation (ISLR 3.1.3). Dividing RSE by the mean response gives a rough percentage error. Even the true regression line would miss by about σ\sigmaσ; RSE is the noise level no straight line can remove.
