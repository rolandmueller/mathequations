Information Gain

IG=H(parent)−∑c∈{L, R}ncn H(c)\htmlClass{term-ig}{\textcolor{#8b5cf6}{\mathrm{IG}}} = \htmlClass{term-parent}{\textcolor{#10b981}{H(\text{parent})}} - \htmlClass{term-sum}{\textcolor{#ec4899}{\sum_{c \in \{L,\, R\}}}} \htmlClass{term-weight}{\textcolor{#3b82f6}{\frac{n_c}{n}}} \, \htmlClass{term-child}{\textcolor{#ef4444}{H(c)}}IG=H(parent)−c∈{L,R}∑​nnc​​H(c)

Information gain scores a candidate split: how much does it reduce the class mix? Start from the entropy of the node before splitting and subtract the entropy of each child node, summed over the left and right child and weighted by the share of observations that land there. The tree tries every predictor and cut point and takes the split with the largest gain.

Read aloud: I G equals H of the parent minus the sum over the children c in L and R of n c over n times H of c.

Definitions

ig

Information gain IG\mathrm{IG}IG. Pronounced: "I G", or "the information gain". Always ≥0\ge 0≥0: splitting never increases weighted entropy. Growing stops when no split gains enough (or by max_depth\texttt{max\_depth}max_depth, min_samples_leaf\texttt{min\_samples\_leaf}min_samples_leaf), and pruning removes splits that did not pay off on new data. The same formula with Gini in place of HHH is the "decrease in impurity" that MDI feature importance adds up per feature. Information gain favours features with many distinct values, which can shatter the data into tiny pure nodes; the gain ratio (C4.5) divides by the entropy of the split itself to correct for this. Binary trees such as scikit-learn's CART are less affected but still biased.

parent

Entropy of the parent node H(parent)H(\text{parent})H(parent). Pronounced: "H of the parent". The class mix before the split. It is the same for every candidate split of this node, so maximizing the gain means minimizing the weighted child entropy.

sum

Sum over child nodes ∑c∈{L, R}\sum_{c \in \{L,\, R\}}∑c∈{L,R}​. Pronounced: "the sum over the children c in L and R". LLL and RRR are the left and right child, e.g. "income < 50k" and "income ≥ 50k". Multiway splits just have more children.

weight

Child weight ncn\frac{n_c}{n}nnc​​. Pronounced: "n c over n". ncn_cnc​ is the number of observations sent to child ccc; nnn is the number in the parent. Weighting stops the tree from favouring a split that carves off one tiny pure node while leaving the rest as mixed as before.

child

Entropy of child ccc, H(c)H(c)H(c). Pronounced: "H of c". Computed with the same entropy formula from the class shares inside the child. Worked example: a parent with 10 positives and 10 negatives has H=1H = 1H=1 bit.
