Bootstrap Hypothesis Test

To test whether the true mean could be the hypothesized value mu zero, first build a world where the null hypothesis holds: shift every observation so the data keep their shape but have mean mu zero instead of the observed mean. Then bootstrap from the shifted data, compute the mean of each resample, and count how often it is at least as large as the observed mean. That share, summed over B resamples and divided by B, is the p-value.

Read aloud: x tilde sub i equals x sub i minus x bar plus mu zero. p hat equals one over B times the sum from b equals one to B of the indicator that x bar star b is at least x bar.
