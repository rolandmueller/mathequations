The ECDF answers: what fraction of the data is at most a given value x? Count the observations that are less than or equal to it, over all n data points, and divide by n. Plotted, it is a staircase that climbs from 0 to 1, with one step of height 1/n per observation.

Read aloud: F hat n of x equals one over n times the sum from i equals one to n of the indicator that x sub i is at most x.
