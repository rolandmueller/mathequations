One-Sample t-Test

t=xˉ−μ0s/n\htmlClass{term-t}{\textcolor{#8b5cf6}{t}} = \frac{\htmlClass{term-mean}{\textcolor{#10b981}{\bar{x}}} - \htmlClass{term-null}{\textcolor{#ec4899}{\mu_0}}}{\htmlClass{term-sd}{\textcolor{#3b82f6}{s}} / \htmlClass{term-root}{\textcolor{#ef4444}{\sqrt{n}}}}t=s/n​xˉ−μ0​​

The t-statistic asks how far the sample mean lies from the value claimed by the null hypothesis, measured in standard errors: the spread of the data shrunk by the square root of the sample size. A value near 0 is what chance alone would produce; a value beyond about ±2 is surprising if the null hypothesis were true.

Read aloud: t equals x bar minus mu zero, over s divided by root n.

Definitions

t

Test statistic ttt. Pronounced: "t". Under the null hypothesis it follows a ttt distribution with df=n−1df = n - 1df=n−1 degrees of freedom. The p-value is the probability of a ttt at least this extreme under that distribution: scipy.stats.ttest_1samp(x, mu0)\texttt{scipy.stats.ttest\_1samp(x, mu0)}scipy.stats.ttest_1samp(x, mu0). Conditions (ISRS 4.1): independent observations, and either n≥30n \ge 30n≥30 without extreme skew or outliers, or nearly normal data when nnn is small. Without them, use a bootstrap hypothesis test. A small p-value says the data are unlikely under H0H_0H0​; it does not measure how large or important the effect is. Report the difference and its confidence interval too.

mean

Sample mean xˉ\bar{x}xˉ. Pronounced: "x bar". The estimate being tested.

null

Hypothesized mean μ0\mu_0μ0​. Pronounced: "mu zero", or "mu naught". The value stated by the null hypothesis H0:μ=μ0H_0: \mu = \mu_0H0​:μ=μ0​, for example "the average shoe size is 42". It comes from the question, not from the data.

sd

Sample standard deviation sss. Pronounced: "s". Estimated from the data. Using sss instead of the unknown σ\sigmaσ is why the reference distribution is ttt, not normal.

root

Square root of the sample size, n\sqrt{n}n​. Pronounced: "root n". The denominator s/ns/\sqrt{n}s/n​ is the standard error of the mean. Larger samples make the same difference xˉ−μ0\bar{x} - \mu_0xˉ−μ0​ more significant.
