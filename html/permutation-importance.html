Permutation Feature Importance

How much does a model rely on feature j? Shuffle that one column, which breaks its link to the target, and see how much the score drops. The importance is the score on the intact data minus the average over K repeated shuffles of the score with feature j shuffled.

Read aloud: i sub j equals s minus one over K times the sum from k equals one to K of s sub k j.

Definitions

imp

Permutation importance of feature jjj, iji_jij​. Pronounced: "i sub j". A global measure: one number per feature for the whole model. Works for any model, and on held-out data it measures what matters for generalization. sklearn.inspection.permutation_importance\texttt{sklearn.inspection.permutation\_importance}sklearn.inspection.permutation_importance. Caveat: with correlated features, shuffling one creates unrealistic combinations, and the model can fall back on the correlated partner, so both may look unimportant. Drop-column importance (retrain without the feature and compare) answers a different question and is expensive but avoids unrealistic inputs.

base

Reference score sss. Pronounced: "s". The model's score (e.g. accuracy or R2R^2R2) on the unshuffled data, ideally a validation set.

norm

Normalization 1K\frac{1}{K}K1​. Pronounced: "one over K". KKK is the number of repeated shuffles (n_repeats\texttt{n\_repeats}n_repeats). Their spread shows how stable the importance is.

sum

Summation over repetitions ∑k=1K\sum_{k=1}^{K}∑k=1K​. Pronounced: "the sum from k equals one to K".

perm

Score with feature jjj permuted, sk,js_{k,j}sk,j​. Pronounced: "s sub k j". The score after the kkk-th random shuffle of column jjj. Compare with MDI (mean decrease in impurity, the default feature_importances_\texttt{feature\_importances\_}feature_importances_ of tree models), which is computed on the training data and is biased toward features with many unique values, such as IDs or continuous noise.
