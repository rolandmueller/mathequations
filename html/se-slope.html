The standard error of the slope says how much the estimated slope would wobble across repeated samples. It grows with the noise around the line and shrinks when the predictor values are spread widely around their mean: a wide range of x pins the line down better.

Read aloud: the standard error of beta one hat equals sigma hat over the square root of the sum from i equals one to n of x sub i minus x bar, squared.
